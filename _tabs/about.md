I'm Ruud, a data engineer based in the Netherlands. This blog is part of my
portfolio at [ruudjuffermans.nl](https://ruudjuffermans.nl).

At the Noord-Holland police unit I set up data-driven working in a team that
didn't have it yet. I mapped the data sources, built pipelines in Python and
SQL, designed the data models and delivered the reports that detectives and
managers now steer by. All of that happens under strict privacy and
information-security requirements, with data where a wrong conclusion has real
consequences.

Before that I was a data engineer at the Netherlands Aerospace Centre (NLR),
working on COMPASS, an EU project on digital product passports. I led
requirements engineering with partners across Europe, made the architecture
decisions for the data platform and built the MVP in an international Agile
team.

Alongside that work I spent four years creating Udemy courses on Python, SQL
and full-stack development. The same curiosity led me to AI: RAG, agents and
retrieval evaluation, worked out in the [projects](/projects/) on this site.

> I've seen what happens when data and AI work — and what happens when the
> foundation is missing.
